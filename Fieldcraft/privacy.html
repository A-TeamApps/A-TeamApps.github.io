Fieldcraft Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · applies to the Fieldcraft Android app and its web version

Short version: Fieldcraft has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We don't collect, receive or sell any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app only goes online when you ask it to, for example to download a map or a document, or to buy the unlock through Google Play.

What we collect

Nothing. Fieldcraft has no server of its own. The developers never receive your plans, kits, notes, photos, medical ID, contacts, saved places, location or search questions. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only in its own storage on your phone:

  • what you enter: your preparedness plan, kits and checklists, emergency plans and contacts, medical ID, your own guides, Field ID entries and emergency cards, notes, favourites and settings
  • photos you add to kits, guides or Field ID entries
  • maps, documents and books you download or import
  • saved map points and, if you turn it on, your GPS track

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this, except encyclopedia files you downloaded or added: those are ordinary files in your Downloads folder, a folder you chose, or an SD card or USB drive, and stay until you delete them. Back up my data in Settings saves a copy to a file you choose. That file goes wherever you save or share it.

Android may include the app's data in your device's own backup (for example, Google's backup of your phone) if you have that turned on. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Location

If you allow location access, the map, the Emergency "your location" card and the tools use your phone's GPS. Your position is shown and stored on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn off location access in Android settings at any time.

When the app goes online

Fieldcraft works offline. It connects to the internet only when you do one of the following:

  • Download map areas, or turn on the online map (off unless you turn it on). The app requests map tiles from the U.S. Geological Survey (basemap.nationalmap.gov), or from a tile server whose address you type in yourself. Like any website, that server sees your IP address and which map tiles are requested.
  • Store and book links. "Search to buy" in Kits & checklists and the Recommended books buttons only open a plain search or page in your browser. Only the search words you see are sent to the store, bookshop or library you pick, by your browser. Fieldcraft shares nothing else with them, uses no affiliate or tracking codes, and earns nothing from these links.
  • Get encyclopedias. When you open this screen, the app reads the public catalog at library.kiwix.org, and when you tap Download it fetches the chosen encyclopedia file from download.kiwix.org or the mirror it points to, and saves it into Downloads/Fieldcraft on your phone (visible in your Files app, and kept if you uninstall Fieldcraft) or into a folder you choose instead, which can be on an SD card or USB drive. Those servers see your IP address like any website. Words you type in the search box on that screen are sent to library.kiwix.org to search its catalog. Nothing else is sent, and nothing runs until you open the screen or tap Download. The app checks whether your connection is metered (network state permission). If you are on mobile data or a metered network when you tap Download or Resume, it asks you first, for that download only, and otherwise waits for Wi-Fi. A download that moves to mobile data pauses and asks again. A download only continues by itself while Fieldcraft is open.
  • Save official guides, download a classic handbook or open a source. The app downloads the page or document, one at a time or a whole category, from the publisher's own website, such as a government agency, or the public-domain handbook EPUB from Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org or its official mirrors). A web page is loaded the way a browser loads it, so that website sees a normal visit. When you save official guides on mobile data or a metered network, the app asks first.
  • Open an outside link, such as a source's website. The link opens in your browser and that site's own privacy policy applies.
  • Send a photo to another app. If you choose "Open photo in another app", Android hands the photo to the identification app you pick, such as Seek or Google Lens. That app's own privacy policy applies.

None of these requests includes your personal data.

Free trial and purchase (Google Play version)

The Google Play version is free to try for 7 days, then a one-time purchase unlocks it. The trial is counted from the day the app was installed on your phone. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Fieldcraft only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the unlock. We never see your name, email address or payment details, and the app keeps no record of you beyond "unlocked" on your phone.

Permissions

  • Location: GPS on the map and emergency screens, as described above.
  • Notifications and background work: show progress and keep a download or import you started running with the screen off.
  • Internet: only for the actions listed above.
  • Network state: to tell whether you are on mobile data or a metered network, so it can ask before downloading.

Fieldcraft does not ask for camera, photo-library, contacts or storage permission. When you add a photo, Android's own camera or photo picker opens, and only the photo you pick is given to the app. The flashlight and SOS light tools use the camera flash without needing camera access.

Children

Fieldcraft is a general-audience reference app made with Scouting families in mind. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
